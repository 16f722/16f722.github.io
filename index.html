I designed a ladder logic program and implemented in MicroPython. I prepared revisions and programmed the controllers in the field as requirements shifted. I selected the fabricated embedded approach to have a flexible and feasible solution while I met the need to be compact, economical, and maintainable.

MicroPython Access Control System for Wiegand Badge Readers

I wanted a deeper understanding of card reader communication and developed GPIO interrupt routines on Raspberry Pico for Wiegand signals (still commonly used) and reconstruct the fob id number, from different card readers and card types. I used an LCD to display the recovered Wiegand data. I then implemented a primative persistend sorted database of names, apartment, fob id, and access level which is used to authentic fobs presented to a badage reader and activate the card reader LED and lock relay as appropriate.
