Transmission Line Buffer

During commissioning of vehicle gate access control, I found invalid reads for the far range (clicker) receiver while the keypad operated correctly. After inspecting the invalid fob values from the controller log, I used an oscillscope to confirm excessive cross talk in the cable run (single ended signaling over 400ft). I leveraged Gemini to develop a simple two transistor buffer with a threshold well above the cross talk and high gain to speed up the slew rate. I assembled two dual channel boards, used a saw tooth wave to valid the implementation, and completed they access control install. I then used KiCad to capture a PCB layout, submitted to JLCPCB for SMT assembly, populated headers, and validated the production run for future projects.

MicroPython Access Control System for Wiegand Badge Readers

link: Pico ACS

I wanted a deeper understanding of card reader communication and developed GPIO interrupt routines on Raspberry Pico to reconstruct the fob id number from Wiegand signals (still commonly used), for different card readers and card types. I used an LCD to display the recovered Wiegand data. I then implemented a primative persistend sorted database of names, apartment, fob id, and access level which is used to authentic fobs presented to a badage reader and activate the card reader LED and lock relay as appropriate.
